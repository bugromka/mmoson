What to write about

MMOSO'N is a personal engineering and research project. Open questions about calculations, comments on the publication and cooperation proposals. Below — four reasons to write, and what you can get in return.

🛰️
Calculations and verification
Questions about shell strength, thermal and energy balance, axis-to-rim transport, biosphere and food. I'll answer in substance or show where the model has a gap.
🤝
Cooperation
Joint research, student and school papers on the station topic, popularisation, publishing and media proposals.
📄
Use of materials
The project is distributed under «All rights reserved». Quotation, reprinting, translation and inclusion in other projects require written consent of the author — send your request by email.
🔍
Errors and comments
Found inconsistency of numbers, units or links in the publication — report it. A comment indicating the document and section is processed fastest.

What can be discussed

🛠️ Load-bearing shell and strength 🌡️ Heat rejection and power 🚡 Transport and docking 🌱 Biosphere and food ⚖️ Law and station structure 📚 Publication and citation

The project is open to substantive criticism: a calculation that doesn't add up is more important than a compliment. But I keep the boundaries of claims honest — the station remains a calculation model, not an agreed and built object.
