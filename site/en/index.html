About the project

Island Three · Earth–Sun L1 Lagrange point

MMOSO'N is a personal engineering and research project: a calculable model of a habitable cylindrical orbital station, derived from first principles. Not an illustration of the future, but a set of figures that add up.

Configuration — classic Island Three: two counter-rotating cylinders with a radius of 5 km. Rotation provides 1.0 g gravity on the rim. The cylinders are not joined along their side surfaces — this is physically impossible under counter-rotation; connection runs only through the axes via bearing units.

The station's axis remains fixed in inertial space. All external equipment — docking ports, radiators, antennas, solar wings — is placed only on the two end frames. The internal volume is illuminated by reactor-driven axial light-heat beams; there are no mirrors or solar sails.
