Key results and limits of the calculation model

  • Table 5.2 Annual need is 1.022 trillion kcal and 25.6 thousand tons of protein; the scenario inventory target is 1.533 trillion kcal and 33.2 thousand tons. Gross annual system output is 2.338 trillion kcal and 87.2 thousand tons; modelled net output after an aggregate assumed 20% deduction is 1.870 trillion kcal and 69.8 thousand tons. ×1.83 and ×2.73 are annual net-output-to-annual-need ratios—not stock sizes or prescribed constant production levels.
  • Section 29 Table 5.4 reports 2.216 trillion kcal of gross crop output; the approximately 0.122-trillion-kcal difference from whole-system gross output requires reconciliation against livestock and aquaculture. The modelled protein check is also incomplete.
  • Table 29.2 The preliminary scenario with annual net-output floors of ×1.15 for calories and ×1.20 for protein gives about 463 km² of cropland and a conditional 344 km² release. At the minimum-area solution, protein output remains about ×2.49 of need: ×1.20 is a lower floor, not the actual output. This scenario does not approve land-use or inventory policy.
  • Section 34 The cylinders’ side surface is 2,513.2 km². Comparisons with Moscow, Cape Town, Tokyo Metropolis, Ottawa and Jacksonville use administrative or regional territories; those areas are not equivalent to built-up footprints or functional urban areas. The 120 m²/person value is a gross planning-site allowance, not measured building area.
  • Section 4.16 Waterproof hull protection from precipitation and drainage, secondary leak collection and removable, replaceable modules are project requirements. Design, materials, loads and maintenance need further engineering; verified watertightness is not claimed.
