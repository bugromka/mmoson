Dietary need, food flows and limits of the calculation model

  • Tables 5.1–5.2 For 1,000,000 people, the scenario uses equal sex and age-group weights (18–29.9 / 30–59.9 years), BMI 21 and PAL 1.75. The FAO/WHO/UNU mean is 2,525 kcal/person/day, or 0.921625 trillion kcal/year. This is a planning estimate, not an individual medical prescription; FAO/WHO/UNU source.
  • Stock reference Protein is a separate assumption of 70 g/person/day, or 25,550 t/year. Scenario stock references of ×1.5 for energy and ×1.3 for protein are 1.3824375 trillion kcal and 33,215 t. They are stocks, not annual production; product mix, shelf life, losses and rotation are not agreed.
  • Consumers The station population is the primary intended consumer of food products. Grains and tubers are candidate food ingredients, legumes a dietary protein source, and oilseeds an oil source; press cake and other by-products could enter feed or compost only after sanitary and nutrient checks. Diet composition and channel shares remain unset.
  • Archived scenario · Tables 5.4–5.5 The former 807 km² crop area and 2.216 trillion kcal crop output are retained for traceability only. The former 150 km² maize allocation reflected an assumed 4,380 kcal/m²/year and a starch-feedstock role; it is not an approved share of a future plan. Areas and output have not been recalculated.
  • Waste and secondary streams The 547,500 t/year flow and 60/25/15 partition are scenario assumptions, not measured composition or an approved processing circuit. 115 thousand t/year is substrate for black soldier fly trials, not formulated feed; biomass output, losses and nutrient return are unverified.
  • Open reconciliation Former fish-flow entries of 25,000 and 70,000 t/year differ by 45,000 t; the former protein discrepancy is 2,020 t. The aggregate 20% loss deduction has not been validated by product or process stage. No production plan is calculated until nutrients, edible fractions, feed and product flows are reconciled.
  • Section 34 Comparisons use real cities; administrative or regional areas are not automatically equivalent to built-up footprints or functional urban areas. The 120 m²/person figure is a gross planning-site allowance, not measured building area.
  • Figure 23 and quarantine A flow of 100,000 people/year with a 40-day holding period corresponds to 10,951 concurrent places; transport throughput alone does not determine the settlement rate.
  • Section 4.16 and Figure 24 Waterproof hull protection from precipitation and drainage, secondary leak collection and removable, replaceable modules are requirements. Figure 24’s panel-size comparison is illustrative, not a working design; structure, loads and maintenance need separate engineering.

All areas and outputs from the former crop scenario are archived, not approved. Detailed assumptions, limitations and references appear in the combined Edition 8 documents in Russian and English.

Operational lessons from real stations—and limits of transfer

  • Salyut-7 and Mir Manual rendezvous, emergency docking and restoration work are concrete examples of failure preparedness—not proof that every component is repairable. Salyut-7 rescue · last Mir expedition (Russian).
  • ISS / NASA Maintainability and life extension require access, diagnostics, tools, training, spares and verification. ISS maintenance analysis shows a trade: replacing a complete ORU saves crew repair time but requires spares and logistics volume; lower-level repair may reduce stowage but takes more crew time. NASA’s roughly 98% water-recovery result refers to the U.S.-segment system with brine processing—not the entire station water balance. NASA maintainability brief · ISS life extension · water recovery.
  • SpaceX Dragon / ESA ATV Automated rendezvous and docking are paired with monitoring, crew training and rehearsed contingency procedures. Crew-2 mission · ATV docking training.
  • Application to MMOSO'N Transferable principles are module isolation, independent control, maintainability, stream-specific water accounting, docking monitoring and planned maintenance. ISS integration history (Russian).

These are operational precedents for small crews in low Earth orbit with ground support and resupply. They do not validate scaling to one million inhabitants, a fully closed biosphere, kilometre-scale cylinders or autonomous operations near L1. MMOSO'N performance values require independent analysis, testing and certification.
