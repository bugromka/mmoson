Engineering

Six circuits, each closed on calculation

Load-bearing shell

External pressure 152.6 kPa plus self-weight 222.4 kPa. Hoop stress 1,308 MPa, safety margin 1.53 to ultimate strength.

Thermal regulation

Two loops: high-temperature reactor heat rejection 882 GW at 800 °C on 12.4 km² and low-temperature 677 GW at 60 °C on 1,022 km².

Power

Reactor generation 721.4 GW electrical. Axial light-heat beams are the only source of light inside the volume.

Axis-to-rim transport

Funicular as the main route, stairs as a mandatory duplicate, lift for urgent and cargo tasks. Two inclined shafts per cylinder, route 7,429 m, 17.8 min from dock to rim at constant 10° incline.

Docking and locks

Docking on the stationary frame at zero velocity: dock → locks and quarantine → rotating pressure transition → hollow axial shaft → inclined shafts → habitable surface.

Biosphere and food

Zonal crop distribution, food balance, oxygen cycle over centuries, soil profile as an engineering system, closed-population genetics.

Why cylinders are not joined to each other

Side surfaces rotate counter to each other. Any rigid link between them is impossible: connection runs only through the axes via bearing units. Inter-axis distance 21 km, free vacuum gap 11 km, no longitudinal elements between shells.
